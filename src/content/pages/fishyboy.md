## short artist bio

[Giovani Baroni](/giovani-baroni) is a Brazilian multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and producer based in Mexico City. After more than a decade in São Paulo's independent scene — including the alternative rock project sukya \|\| porno — he went solo, writing long songs built around detailed arrangements instead of quick hooks.
His debut album, [bLU](/blu), arrives in September 2026: a 10-track conceptual record, sung across Portuguese, English, and Spanish, meant to be heard front to back. Musically it sits somewhere between Brazilian shoegaze, progressive rock, and psychedelia, tied together by a recurring underwater world.

## short single description

"Fishy Boy" is the second single from [bLU](/blu), [Giovani Baroni](/giovani-baroni)'s debut solo album. At five minutes, it is one of the album's most immersive pieces: an aquatic, hypnotic track built around a clean guitar arpeggio, water sounds, synths, and a groove that doesn't let go.

The song was written years earlier in São Paulo with Baroni's brother and a close friend after a swim, inspired by Radiohead's "Weird Fishes" and the spirit of Modest Mouse. It was almost lost to a shelved project before finding its place near the end of bLU. The lyrics move between English and Portuguese, between the surface and what's underneath, and the physical sensation of being submerged.
