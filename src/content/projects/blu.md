bLU is a debut solo album by Giovani Baroni.

It began as an instinct — to follow water wherever it leads. Into the body, into memory, into the unconscious space where transformation happens before it has a name.

The album moves through psychedelic rock, shoegaze, and experimental songwriting. It borrows from the ocean without trying to explain it. It is, above all, a feeling.
